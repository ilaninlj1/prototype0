What happens under the water?

These are playable. Click and drag up from the water on any phone to dive, then try it. The card and songs are fake, this is only about the feel.

A

Different waters

Drag sideways between four waters. Each plays a different kind of song, and your sea on Home turns that color.

B

Pick a fish

One reef. Each creature is a kind of song: turtle for slow, puffer for loud. The one you pick swims in your sea on Home.

C

Go deeper

Keep dragging up to sink. The sun fades, and the deeper you go, the fewer people have heard the songs. The sea floor is Buried.
