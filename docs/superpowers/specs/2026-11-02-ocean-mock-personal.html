Your ocean is made of what you did

Tap any creature and it tells you why it looks that way. Switch to Night and watch who wakes up. The jellyfish called "The same song" is in both grown oceans. The songs here are made up.

Day one

Nothing here is yours yet. The sun and water are beautiful, the life is pale. Save a song and it hatches in color.

Someone who listens at night

"Mostly comes out at night. Big, old jellyfish and turtles. Blue reef, because most of what they hear is in minor keys. Huge dunes: they skip a lot."

Someone who listens in the morning

"Up with the sun. Fast little fish and warm coral. Not much sand: they keep most of what they hear."
